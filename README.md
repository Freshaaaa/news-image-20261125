# Wednesday 25 November 2026: 38 stories, 6 regions, 10 fields — 720,000 Russian dead, 5 ships hit in 12 days, nuclear threat

A one-page newspaper poster summing up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026: 38 stories across 6 regions and 10 fields, drawn fresh with Python and Pillow from verified reporting (Reuters, CNN, Mediazona, Meduza, Kyiv Independent, NY Post, FXStreet).

The hottest story leads: 720,000 Russian soldiers dead, one every four minutes, leaked Rosstat data show. Then five Russian vessels hit in 12 days — Zelensky confirms the Gulf of Finland strike on Sparta II and the Merkury corvette sunk off Benghazi — and Medvedev's threat of a preventive nuclear attack. Every lead is drawn as a full illustrated scene, and five more stories get their own scenes in "The Day in Pictures": the Houthi strike on a Saudi refinery and a Riyadh tower, the Sahel drone war, tankers hit near the Strait of Hormuz, the revived Great Barrier Reef, and England's cricket win over Australia.

`build-image.py` renders the poster deterministically from the story data embedded in it — no network, no fetched data at build time. `main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory.
