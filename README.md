# Wednesday 25 November 2026: 28 stories, 6 regions, 10 fields — 720,000 Russian dead, 5 ships hit in 12 days, nuclear threat

A one-page news poster summing up the world news of Wednesday 25 November 2026: 28 stories across 6 regions and 10 fields, drawn fresh with Python/PIL from verified reporting (Reuters, CNN, Mediazona, Meduza, Kyiv Independent, NY Post, FXStreet).

Three leads anchor the day: 720,000 Russian soldiers dead, one every four minutes, leaked Rosstat data show; five Russian vessels hit in 12 days — Zelensky confirms the Gulf of Finland strike on Sparta II and the Merkury corvette sunk off Benghazi; and Medvedev threatens a preventive nuclear attack. Each lead is drawn as a full scene — a memorial field, the war at sea, and a missile under a red warning sky — instead of flat icons.

`main.ts` (Node 22, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory. `build-image.py` draws the poster deterministically from the story data embedded in it — no network, no fetched data at build time.
